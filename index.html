Popular Attractions

Mt. Washington Summit

The highest peak in the Northeastern U.S. notorious for its erratic and unpredictable weather.

Kancamagus Highway

34-mile scenic byway considered one of the best fall foliage drives in the U.S.

Flume Gorge

A natural gorge extending 800 ft at the base of Mount Liberty in Franconia Notch State Park.

Travel Tips

cloud

Prep for Weather

Check the forecast before visiting. Always bring warm clothing and good rain gear. It may be sunny below but cold in the mountains.

pets

Beware of Bears

Store food in closed/locked vehicles or bear containers. Keep a clean campsite so bears don't forage, for their safety and yours.

footprint

Leave No Trace

Leave nature as you found it. Practice responsible, sustainable outdoor recreation to protect these areas for future generations.
